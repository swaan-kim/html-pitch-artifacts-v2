Generate one square transparent PNG with four isolated cute pixel-art animal work partners, exactly centered in each equal quadrant with generous transparent padding, each subject fully within its own quadrant and same apparent scale.

Top right: friendly chestnut small pony, cream muzzle, dark brown mane and visible pony tail, sitting behind a small silver laptop. Make it clearly a pony, not a dog or fox.
Bottom left: friendly warm brown owl, round eyes and small folded wings, holding a small magnifying glass and looking curious.
Bottom right: cream white rabbit with long ears, holding a short paper document and a green pen.
All four use consistent chunky pixel silhouettes, minimal muted sage/cream/brown palette, clear black or dark brown outlines matching the reference, simple front-facing seated poses, warm calm expressions. Small props clearly communicate their role. Each animal occupies roughly 65 percent of its cell width and height. No labels, text, typography, borders, cards, drop shadows, scenery or watermarks. Actual alpha transparency, not checkerboard. These are all character sprites for ONE sprite sheet.
